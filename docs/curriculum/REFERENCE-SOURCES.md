# Curriculum Reference Sources and Rights Register

This register identifies public, no-API references for curriculum design and editorial review. These resources inform the method; they are **not** a content feed and do not authorize copying or republishing protected material.

## Primary framework

### Council of Europe — CEFR Companion Volume (2020)

- Official reference: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-companion-volume-and-its-language-versions
- Descriptor index: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors
- Use: define learner outcomes as observable can-do statements; cover reception, production, interaction and mediation; distinguish Pre-A1, A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 and C2.
- Important: CEFR describes communicative ability, not a fixed grammar syllabus or a proprietary vocabulary list. Map grammar and vocabulary to outcomes rather than treating grammar points as CEFR levels.

## Publisher and dictionary references

### Oxford Learner's Dictionaries — Oxford 3000 / Oxford 5000 / Phrase List

- Word lists: https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/wordlists/
- Oxford 3000/5000 methodology: https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/about/wordlists/oxford3000-5000
- Use: candidate vocabulary prioritisation by frequency, learner relevance and CEFR alignment; check useful meanings and phrases.
- Scope: Oxford 3000 is described as core learner vocabulary aligned A1–B2; Oxford 5000 adds advanced vocabulary at B2–C1. This is a reference for selection, not a complete course syllabus.
- Rights: do not copy or redistribute the full proprietary word lists, definitions, example sentences or dictionary content without permission.

### Cambridge English — A1 Movers preparation

- Official preparation page: https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/qualifications/young-learners/paper/movers/preparation/
- Exam overview: https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/movers/
- Use: inspect task families and assessment balance; free preparation resources include wordlists, sample papers, listening samples and speaking-test videos/commentary.
- Scope warning: A1 Movers is designed for young learners. Use it as an A1 task-format reference, not as the complete adult/general-English syllabus or a source of copied questions.
- Rights: do not copy sample-paper items, audio, images, videos or examiner materials into this product.

### Longman / Pearson — Longman learner dictionaries

- Pearson overview: https://www.pearson.com/languages/educators/connected-english-learning-program/longman-english-dictionaries.html
- Free dictionary site identified by Pearson: https://www.ldoceonline.com/
- Use: editorial verification of natural examples, common usage, grammar patterns, word families, collocations and register. Prefer teaching phrases and chunks in context over isolated translation pairs.
- Rights: dictionary access is not a licence to scrape, reproduce definitions, corpus examples or collocation entries.

## Free supplementary skills practice

### British Council — LearnEnglish A1

- A1 level overview: https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/level/understand-your-level/a1-elementary
- A1 listening: https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/listening/a1
- A1 reading: https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/reading/a1
- A1–A2 grammar: https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2
- Use: inspect the range of real-world A1 situations and the structure of preparation → input → comprehension tasks; identify gaps in our listening, reading, speaking and writing coverage.
- Access note: British Council states that LearnEnglish materials are free to access. Its site is a collection of activities rather than a sequenced course and does not itself provide learner progress tracking.
- Rights: link learners to the original resource; do not embed, download, copy or redistribute recordings, transcripts, illustrations or exercises unless the relevant licence expressly permits it.

## Implementation and rights policy

1. All lesson prompts, dialogues, examples, explanations and assessment items committed to the repository must be original or separately licensed for reuse.
2. Public access, a free download, or a free website does not by itself grant redistribution rights.
3. Do not add a runtime dependency on these sites, scrape them, or require their APIs. The app's core lesson delivery and deterministic grading must work offline from its own versioned D1 content.
4. Use external references for manual editorial review and links for optional learner enrichment.
5. Keep answer keys and grading rules server-side. Public lesson responses expose only published prompts/options allowed by the API contract.
6. A unit is not complete when it contains only multiple-choice recognition questions. Release planning must separately cover listening, reading, productive writing, speaking/interaction and cumulative assessment.
