# A1 Foundations — Unit 8: Weather, Clothes and Everyday Plans

**Status:** Authored curriculum increment  
**Target:** CEFR A1  
**Prerequisite:** Units 1–7  
**Language pair:** Persian support / English target  
**Content rights:** Original instructional text written for EnglishCodeApp. Oxford, Cambridge and Longman are methodological references only.

## Instructional design

- **CEFR alignment:** understand familiar words about weather, seasons and clothes; describe simple conditions and make basic everyday choices.
- **Vocabulary:** weather adjectives, seasons/months, clothing, colours and common outdoor activities.
- **Grammar and functions:** it is + adjective, present continuous for what someone is wearing now, adjective + noun, let's + base verb, simple present for general preferences.
- **Method:** weather icons and clothing images → lexical retrieval → modelled descriptions → controlled grammar practice → choose-an-activity scenario.
- **Assessment:** four deterministic recognition questions per lesson. Listening to forecasts and spontaneous spoken planning need separate skill tasks.

## Unit outcomes

By the end of this unit, a learner can:
1. describe common weather conditions;
2. name seasons and familiar months;
3. name everyday clothes and colours;
4. ask and answer what someone is wearing;
5. choose simple clothes and activities for familiar weather.

## Lesson 1 — Weather Today

**Can-do:** I can describe the weather today.

**Core vocabulary:** sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy, snowy, hot, warm, cool, cold, weather.

**Language focus:** What's the weather like?; It's sunny; It's cold today.

**Model dialogue:**
> A: What's the weather like today?  
> B: It's cloudy and cool.  
> A: Is it raining?  
> B: No, it isn't.

**Teaching notes:** Use clear weather symbols. Separate temperature words from weather-condition words and avoid presenting regional climate as universal.

**Communicative task:** Choose a weather icon and say two short sentences about it.

## Lesson 2 — Seasons and Months

**Can-do:** I can name the seasons and recognise months.

**Core vocabulary:** spring, summer, autumn/fall, winter; January to December.

**Language focus:** in summer; in December; My favourite season is spring; It is cold in winter.

**Model dialogue:**
> A: What's your favourite season?  
> B: I like spring.  
> A: Why?  
> B: It is warm.

**Teaching notes:** Teach autumn and fall as regional alternatives. Seasons and weather patterns vary by hemisphere and climate; avoid fixed assumptions about local months.

**Communicative task:** Match seasonal pictures to words and choose a favourite season.

## Lesson 3 — Clothes and Colours

**Can-do:** I can name common clothes and describe their colours.

**Core vocabulary:** T-shirt, shirt, trousers, jeans, skirt, dress, coat, jacket, shoes, boots, hat, scarf, socks; red, blue, green, yellow, black, white.

**Language focus:** a red coat; blue shoes; What colour is it?; They are black.

**Model dialogue:**
> A: What colour is your jacket?  
> B: It's dark blue.  
> A: Is it new?  
> B: Yes, it is.

**Teaching notes:** Use inclusive clothing images and accept different clothing conventions. Teach colour before adjective+noun combinations.

**Communicative task:** Describe three clothing items in a picture using colour + noun.

## Lesson 4 — What Are You Wearing?

**Can-do:** I can ask and answer what someone is wearing now.

**Core vocabulary:** wear, put on, take off, shirt, coat, shoes, hat, scarf.

**Language focus:** What are you wearing?; I am wearing a jacket; She is wearing boots; They are wearing coats.

**Model dialogue:**
> A: What are you wearing today?  
> B: I'm wearing jeans and a green shirt.  
> A: Are you wearing a coat?  
> B: No, I'm not.

**Teaching notes:** Present continuous is introduced as a useful description of what someone has on now. Contrast the question form with short affirmative statements; avoid teaching every spelling rule at once.

**Communicative task:** Describe a character in a picture; a partner identifies the character.

## Lesson 5 — Weather and Everyday Plans

**Can-do:** I can choose suitable clothes and simple activities for the weather.

**Core vocabulary:** umbrella, sunglasses, raincoat, warm coat, park, beach, walk, swim, stay at home.

**Language focus:** It's raining; Take an umbrella; Let's go to the park; I wear a coat when it is cold.

**Model dialogue:**
> A: It's sunny today.  
> B: Let's go to the park.  
> A: Good idea.  
> B: Take your sunglasses!

**Teaching notes:** Teach let's + base verb as a fixed invitation pattern. Discuss practical choices without implying that all learners have the same access to activities or equipment.

**Communicative task:** Choose one of three weather cards and suggest suitable clothes and one activity.
